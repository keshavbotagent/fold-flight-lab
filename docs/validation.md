# Validation record

Solver **`2.2.0-indoor-guide`** passes 39 automated physics, experiment, catalogue and shipped-data tests. The Chromium browser suite passes 14 groups of checks at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 pixels, including both complete search objectives, indoor/outdoor presets, all fold guides and mobile layout. A targeted check verifies cancellation through the browser tool and resetting the clock when the ranking objective changes. Run `npm test` and, with Vite running on port 5173, `node scripts/browser-qa.mjs` to reproduce the full suite; use `--mobile-only` for the targeted mobile checks.

These checks validate equations, numerical integration, consistency and interface behavior. They do not establish agreement with measured paper-plane flights. Aerodynamic coefficients, aerodynamic-center/center-of-gravity positions, damping derivatives and inertia factors are estimates; folding errors, flexibility, humidity effects on paper properties and detailed separated flow remain outside this model.

| Independent control or invariant | What the tests establish |
|---|---|
| Constant-gravity vacuum | With explicit isolation options, positions and velocities match `x=vx₀t` and `y=h+vy₀t−gt²/2`; the landing time matches the positive quadratic root. A body initially at rest retains its attitude while gravity changes its velocity. |
| Principal-axis vacuum rotation | Quaternions follow the analytical constant-rate rigid-body solution and remain unit length. |
| Nonprincipal vacuum rotation | Euler gyroscopic coupling changes body rates while rotational energy and world angular momentum remain constant to a relative tolerance of 5 × 10⁻⁶. |
| Atmosphere | Sea-level pressure, density, gravity and Sutherland viscosity match reference values; temperature and elevation produce the expected trends. |
| Humidity and pressure | Buck saturation vapor pressure and moist-air density match independent reference values. Zero humidity gives the exact dry-air limit; humidity lowers density and increasing sea-level pressure raises it. Celsius/Kelvin and hPa/Pa conversions, input bounds, finite diagnostics, deterministic flights and the complete New Delhi preset are checked. |
| Absolute altitude and ground | Ground elevation plus above-ground height determines mean-sea-level altitude exactly once; equivalent absolute altitudes and temperatures yield identical atmospheres. Locations below sea level have the expected higher pressure and density. An isolated drop on a 5,000 m field lands at zero local height and agrees with the local-gravity analytical limit. Ordinary aerodynamic flights also respond to ground elevation. |
| Aerodynamic forces | Total effective drag obeys `D=½ρV²SCd`, including dissipative sideslip projection. Force power equals `−D·V` in still air, including stalled and reverse-flow cases. |
| Aerodynamic moments | Pitch error produces a restoring moment; damping opposes pitch rate; mirrored sideslip produces mirrored restoring yaw and signed dihedral roll, including the reversed roll response of Sky King’s anhedral wings. |
| Mechanical passivity | Exact inverse-square gravitational potential is used in energy checks. Translational and total mechanical energy decrease between samples; instantaneous force-plus-moment work stays nonpositive. A 1,056-state envelope chooses the angular rate that maximizes static-moment-plus-linear-damping power and still finds no positive net aerodynamic power. |
| Integration refinement | Selected flights are repeated at 120, 240, 480 and 960 Hz. Optimized launch replays retain the winning design at a finer step. The benchmark also performs complete equal-budget searches at three steps. |
| Experiment integrity | All designs use equal paper mass and identical held conditions, including temperature, elevation, humidity and sea-level pressure. Searches have equal exhaustive budgets, deterministic seeds, monotonic progress and working cancellation. Time-capped observations remain marked. |
| Champion catalogue and geometry | Three documented achievements retain their distinct categories and sources; removing that metadata cannot change a flight. Every design has a matching illustrated guide. New meshes have finite vertices, distinct planforms and the expected physical dimensions under the common visual scale. |
| Shipped leaderboard | Every stored row contains the current solver version and complete launch conditions. Current reruns reproduce stored airtime, range and other metrics, and the actual reruns preserve the displayed rank order. |
